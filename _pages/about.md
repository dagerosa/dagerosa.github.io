My name is Daniele Gerosa, and since 2024 I am a postdoctoral fellow at Chalmers University of Technology, Göteborg (Sweden). I belong to the group of [Thomas Eriksson](https://www.chalmers.se/en/persons/thomase/). I received the Ph.D in mathematics from Lund University (Sweden) in 2022, under the supervision of [Marcus Carlsson](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lucat/user/7240ab1d6ba79a9dafc458e7602b150e), and the M.Sc in mathematics from University of Padova (Italy) in 2017, under the supervision of [Roberto Monti](https://www.math.unipd.it/~monti/index.html). Between 2022 and 2024 I was with Ericsson AB, Göteborg, in the algorithm development team.

My research interests are compressive sensing, applied and pure sampling theory, statistical signal processing for communication and radar applications, and more broadly mathematics for signal processing. 

**Statement on LLMs.** In general, I endorse a global halt of (private, at least) AI development. I have signed the [Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics](https://leidendeclaration.ai/), the [open letter to Caltech's Mathaton](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1IL0b2oG2KvvSnxn_DuXsNxuHaCefDJ9hNY1k9BP3kKQ/edit?tab=t.0) and the ["A Severe Misalignment of AI in Mathematics" statement](https://mathandai.org/) written by a number of Fields medallists. Tech behemoths pose significant existential threats to academia, to research and mathematics as *praxis*, to democratic structures and to the environment. I currently *do* make limited use of LLMs in my research activity, although I try to limit my interactions to asking for references and to maieutic discussions. I never ask for one-shot solutions, a practice that I find gross. I set up guardrails to prevent the LLM from outputting "solutions" to problems I am actively working on. I do not use LLMs to perform reviews. I believe prompters should not be listed as co-authors. I believe that mathematicians tightly and actively collaborating with AI companies are accomplices of the deterioration of the discipline.
